# Privacy notice template

A starting point for the notice you give students (and, separately, staff). Replace everything in square brackets, remove what does not apply, and have it reviewed by your privacy lead or counsel. Keep the language plain: the people reading it are students.

---

## How [INSTITUTION] uses imPress in class

**What this is.** In some classes you answer quizzes and polls with a small button module or on a screen. This notice explains what we record when you do.

**Who is responsible.** [INSTITUTION], [address], is responsible for your data. Questions or requests: [privacy contact, e-mail].

**What we record**

- Your enrollment number, name, programme, and the classes you are enrolled in. [E-mail and phone, if you collect them.]
- Which answer you chose to each quiz question or poll, and how long you took.
- Whether you were marked present in a class.
- Which module is linked to you.

We do **not** [record audio or video / track location / use this for anything outside class: edit to match what is true].

**Why we use it.** [To mark quizzes and show results to your teacher / to record attendance / to improve teaching: list the real purposes, each with its legal basis. Examples: public task, legitimate interest, consent: choose with your counsel.]

**Who sees it.** Your teachers see answers and attendance for the classes they teach. Administrators who run the system can see all records. [Vendor name], which [hosts / supports] the system, acts on our instructions. We do not sell your data.

**Where it is kept and for how long.** On servers at [location]. Quiz and poll answers and attendance are kept for [period], after which they are [deleted / anonymised]. Security logs are kept for [period]. Backups are kept for [period].

**Your rights.** You can ask to see the data we hold about you, to have mistakes corrected, to have it erased where the law allows, to object to some uses, and to complain to [supervisory authority / Data Protection Board of India / grievance officer: name and contact]. Write to [privacy contact]. We answer within [30 days / the period the law sets].

**Automated decisions.** The system does not make decisions about you by itself; marks and attendance are reviewed by your teacher. [Edit if untrue.]

**Changes.** We will update this notice if we change how we use your data. This version: [date].
